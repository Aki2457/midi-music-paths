A note about compatibility

KTK’s internal ZIP/JSON layout and 192 timing units per quarter are inferred from sample projects, not a full published specification. MIDI-to-KTK pitch mapping is provisional; app open/save round-trip compatibility has not been verified.

KTK note records do not store MIDI velocity, channel, track name, controllers, pitch bend, or timed program changes. Mid-song tempo, meter, and key changes are rejected rather than flattened. Supported files are Standard MIDI format 0/1 using PPQN timing; excess tracks are combined into the eight available lanes without dropping notes. Auto-instrument mode cannot preserve more than eight distinct instrument groups.
