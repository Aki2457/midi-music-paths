THEN, CHOOSE WHERE TO GO

Two paths, one starting file

Use the same downloaded MIDI for either path.
PATH 01♫

Make a Song Maker song

Turn your MIDI into a playable Chrome Music Lab song and share link.

  1. Open Song BridgeChoose the MIDI you downloaded, or another .mid file.
  2. Check the previewAdjust tempo or grid if you like. The preview happens in your browser.
  3. Create the song linkClick Send MIDI to Google & create link. This is the step that sends the converted MIDI and settings to Google.
Open Song Bridge ↗

Song Maker has no MIDI import button; Song Bridge reshapes the notes for its grid. Long or complex songs may be simplified.

PATH 02▰

Convert to KTK and listen

Make a .ktk project from a MIDI, then open it in the browser player.

  1. Convert the MIDIOpen the KTK Converter, choose your MIDI, keep the recommended Pianica instrument, then choose Convert to .ktk.
  2. Save the projectDownload the .ktk file. Leave it in Downloads so it is easy to find.
  3. Open and playOpen the KTK Player, choose the downloaded .ktk, then press Play.
Open KTK Converter ↗Open KTK Player ↗

The player makes an experimental browser-synth preview, not Katokatone’s original sounds. Pitch and timing may be approximate.

◎

Your file stays on your Chromebook until you choose otherwise

The KTK converter and player work locally in the browser. Song Bridge also previews locally; only clicking its Google-send button sends the converted MIDI and settings to Google’s Song Maker save service to create a link.

IF SOMETHING DOESN’T OPEN

A quick check

Wrong file type? Song Bridge and KTK Converter need a .mid MIDI file. The KTK Player needs a .ktk project—it cannot open MIDI directly.

File not showing? In the Chromebook picker, select Downloads. If the download has not finished, wait and try again.

Unsupported MIDI? Use a Standard MIDI type 0 or 1 file with regular musical timing. Very complex or unusual MIDI files may not convert cleanly.
